Islamic Financial Obligations & Charity — A Guide

Understanding each form of support you can offer

1 & 2

Khums — Sahm-e-Imam & Sahm-e-Sadaat

Khums is a compulsory contribution of 20% on one's annual savings — including cash and bank balance — that have not been used for personal needs over the course of one year.

  • It should be calculated and paid once every year on a fixed date chosen by the individual.
  • This date should remain consistent each year (e.g., every Muharram 1st or one's birthday).
  • Half goes to Sahm-e-Imam ʿAlayh al-Salam (for Islamic institutes and religious causes).
  • Half goes to Sahm-e-Sadaat (for the poor and needy among the descendants of the Prophet ﷺ).
3

Radd-e-Mazalim

Radd-e-Mazalim is an amount given in charity to compensate for unpaid or forgotten debts, especially when:

  • The creditor cannot be traced, or
  • One fears embarrassment or difficulty in returning it directly.

This amount is given in charity on behalf of the rightful owner, freeing one's conscience and account in the hereafter.

4

Zakaat

Zakat is a compulsory charity applicable to specific forms of wealth according to Islamic guidelines:

  • Food grains (wheat, barley, dates, raisins) — upon reaching the prescribed minimum (nisab)
  • Livestock — cattle, sheep/goats, camels

Zakat purifies one's wealth and is among the pillars of Islamic financial obligation.

5

Sadaqah (Voluntary Charity)

Sadaqah should be given whenever needed and without delay, as its impact is immediate and spiritually beneficial.

  • Give daily Sadaqah via QR code — even a small amount each day carries immense reward.
  • Set aside contributions in a box at home and donate monthly or at your convenience.

"Sadaqah extinguishes sin as water extinguishes fire."

— Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) · Thawāb al-Aʿmāl, Sheikh al-Ṣadūq, p. 142 · Also: Al-Kāfī, Vol. 4, p. 3

6

Remembrance for Your Marhoomeen

You may dedicate charity — cash or food grains — for the Isaal-e-Sawab (transfer of spiritual reward) to your deceased loved ones.

  • This act carries immense spiritual reward and benefits them in the hereafter.
  • You may give a certain amount or foodgrain in their name — it reaches them as a gift of light.

This is among the most beautiful acts of love one can offer to a departed soul.

7

General Charity

According to Hadith, even one rupee given in charity to a religious teacher or religious student carries great reward — multiplied many times in the sight of Allah.

  • Supporting an Islamic institution is among the highest forms of Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing charity).
  • Your contribution directly sustains scholars, students, and the dissemination of Islamic knowledge.

"When a person dies, his deeds come to an end except for three: Sadaqah Jariyah, knowledge by which people benefit, or a righteous child who prays for him."

— Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) · Man Lā Yaḥḍuruhu al-Faqīh, Sheikh al-Ṣadūq, Vol. 2, p. 90 · Also: Wasāʾil al-Shīʿa, Vol. 16, p. 289
